## 🖥️ SYSTEM_OVERVIEW
**TONPUMP** is a high-performance terminal designed for high-speed scanning and tracking of Fair Launches on the **TON Blockchain**. It provides real-time data indexing, bonding curve tracking, and security auditing for the most demanding Degen traders.

> **"Scan. Track. Ape. Repeat."** — Engineered by [0✗Block's]

---

## 🚀 CORE_MODULES

### 📡 [TERMINAL]
Real-time indexing of the TON Mainnet. 
- Live tracking of active bonding curves.
- Instant access to purchase pools.
- Visual progress bars for token "graduation".

### 📊 [NETWORK_STATS]
Professional-grade analytics dashboard.
- **Real-Time Volume**: Live distribution of trades across the network.
- **Top Movers**: Automated ranking of high-momentum tokens.
- **Network Health**: Live status of TON gas fees and deployment frequency.

### 📜 [SNIPER_MANUAL]
Your frontline defense against scams (Honeypots, Rugpulls).
- **Mint Authority Check**: Detection of unrenounced mint functions.
- **LP Lock Verification**: Security status of liquidity pools.
- **Wallet Concentration**: Deep scan of top holders to prevent dumps.
